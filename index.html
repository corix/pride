About Us

Celebrating queer joy on the peninsula

Pride of PA is a nonprofit organization based in Port Angeles, Washington. We exist to build visibility, community, and celebration for lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, intersex, asexual, two-spirit, and allied people across Clallam County and the Olympic Peninsula.

Our flagship event is Pride on the Pier, a free, family-friendly festival held each summer on the Port Angeles City Pier at the downtown waterfront.

2026 festival

On the pier

Pride on the Pier is a free, all-ages festival on the Port Angeles City Pier — a day of music, performances, food, and community. It happens because of our vendors, sponsors, and neighbors like you.
