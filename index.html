About Us

Celebrating queer joy on the Olympic Peninsula

Pride of PA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Port Angeles, Washington, serving lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, intersex, asexual, two-spirit, and allied people across Clallam County. We support rural communities in pushing back against isolation and finding strength in solidarity.

Each summer we host Pride on the Pier — a free, family-friendly festival on the Port Angeles City Pier. It's a joyful space where queer individuals, families, and allies can bring their whole selves to the waterfront.

2026 Festival

What you'll find at Pride on the Pier

Pride on the Pier is a free, all-ages festival on the Port Angeles City Pier — music, drag, local food, crafts, and fun activities for all ages. It's all made possible by our sponsors, vendors, volunteers, and neighbors like you.
